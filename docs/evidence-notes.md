# Evidence interpretation

The canonical evidence table is [evidence.tsv](evidence.tsv). Statements concern the
population and evidence level recorded there. Genome annotation, observed sequence
variation and phylogenetic membership are not validated virulence or transmission
scores. No SHAV-specific predictive marker panel for human severity or transmission
was established by this review. No experimental work is part of this project.

Suda et al.’s displayed abstract swaps the SHAV/SATV strain labels. The main text
and Table 1 identify SHAV KSB-6/C/02, Kagoshima 2002, Culicoides spp., as LC741387
(L), LC741388 (M), LC741389 (S). Accession records and main-text tables control
mapping, not the inconsistent abstract.

The French nucleotide and German amino-acid reassortment interpretations differ.
They are not interchangeable and neither establishes a specific donor or direction.
The October 1 second-incursion paper must be considered alongside the initial
European reports. Nomenclature is provisional; project-linked labels are retained
separately from conclusions inferred from this project’s trees.

WOAH’s page displays an October 21 update while discussing the October human case;
that displayed date is recorded without inferring when individual passages changed.
The ECDC and Inserm announcements were checked on October 22. No human-case
accession was identified there. GenBank IHUMI_Shav000001 is explicitly cattle,
not a proxy for the human case. Missing host metadata prevent a proof of absence.
